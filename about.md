My name is **Jiahua Xing** — a PhD candidate and a birder. I keep a China list (currently past 1,150 species), chase targets across the country, and carry a camera for the moments when the bird decides to stay a little longer.

This site is my field journal: trip reports, photographs, and an honest record of what worked, what didn't, and what I'd do differently next time.

## How I travel

Most trips are built around one or two target species. That usually means an early start, a lot of waiting, and a local guide who knows exactly which ridge the owl favours. The notes here are as much for the next birder as they are for me.

## What you'll find here

- **Field notes** — where I went, what I saw, and the logistics that made it possible.
- **Photographs** — birds first, landscapes when the light is too good to ignore.
- **The footprint map** — a growing record of the places the birds have pulled me toward.
